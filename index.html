    How does the marking work?

    Your written answer is checked point by point against the marking scheme, the way an examiner awards marks. Spelling slips and different wording are forgiven. If you think it got one wrong, you can overrule it. Worked maths and diagram labels are self-marked against the solution.

    Higher or Ordinary level?

    Both, wherever the papers exist.

    Do I need to download anything?

    No. It runs in your browser on your phone, tablet or laptop.

    Spotted a mistake?

    Flag it on the question and we'll fix it. Every answer is checked against the marking scheme, but we'd rather hear about a slip than leave it there.

    Is barebones made by the State Exams Commission?

    No. We're independent, made in Ireland, and not affiliated with the SEC.
