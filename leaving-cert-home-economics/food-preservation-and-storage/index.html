    Freezing

    Freezing preserves food by lowering its temperature far enough to stop enzyme action and microbial growth, and by turning free water into ice that micro-organisms cannot use.

    Heat processing destroys micro-organisms and inactivates enzymes. Sealing the food in a clean, airtight container then stops new micro-organisms from entering.
