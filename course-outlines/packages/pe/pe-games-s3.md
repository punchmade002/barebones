## 1. Must cover

- [ ] BD Badminton: grip (forehand and backhand); ready position and return to base; high serve (to the back of the court, deep) and low serve (just over the net, to the front service line), serve hit below the waist; net shots (soft, tumbling); clears (high and deep to the back, to gain time); drop shot (disguised as a clear, lands just over the net); smash (steep, powerful, from a high contact point, racket arm fully extended); tactics: move the opponent around the four corners, attack the weaker side.
- [ ] TN Tennis: continental grip for serve and volley, eastern or semi-western for groundstrokes; serve (ball toss slightly in front, trophy position, contact at full stretch, pronation; variations flat, slice, kick); forehand and backhand drive (early preparation, side-on, low-to-high swing, follow through); volley (short punch, firm wrist, racket head up, move forward); lob (high over a net player); smash (side-on, point at the ball, hit at full extension); tactics: serve and volley, baseline play, attack the weaker side; scoring 15, 30, 40, game.
- [ ] TT Table tennis: shake-hand or penhold grip; legal serve (ball tossed up at least 16 cm from an open palm and struck behind the end line); forehand and backhand drive (topspin, close to the table); push (backspin, soft, keeps the ball low); block (racket angled, absorbs a fast attack close to the table); smash (high ball hit flat and hard); tactics: vary spin and placement; games to 11 points, win by two.
- [ ] VB Volleyball: underarm serve (for beginners) and overarm serve (floating has no spin and moves unpredictably); dig/forearm pass (platform with forearms together, legs bent, push with legs, not swing arms); set/volley (hands above the forehead in a triangle, contact with fingertips, extend legs and arms); spike (approach, two-foot jump, high contact, wrist snap); block (jump with hands above the net, penetrating forward); tactics: dig–set–spike on three touches; rules: three touches maximum, no player touches twice in a row, rally scoring.
- [ ] SQ Squash: ready position on the T; serve (one foot in the service box, ball hits the front wall above the service line and lands in the opposite back quarter); straight drives along the side wall (length to the back); volleys to cut off the ball early; boast (hit onto a side wall first, travels to the front wall); lob (high to the back); tactics: dominate the T, hit to length, move the opponent; let and stroke rules for interference.
- [ ] HD Handball (60 x 30 and 40 x 20 courts): underarm stroke (low, swinging upwards, side-on); overhand stroke (from above the shoulder, for power and kill shots); back wall shot (ball played after it rebounds off the back wall); straight hit from the short line; fly shot (struck before it bounces); side-arm stroke (40 x 20 only); serve (bounced and struck so it lands beyond the short line); use of both hands; tactics: use both hands, kill shots low on the front wall, move the opponent.

## 3. Traps

- In badminton the serve is hit below the waist and diagonally into the opposite service box.
- Volleyball forearm pass power comes from the legs, not swinging the arms.

## 4. What exams ask

- Net/wall games are common contexts (badminton and tennis especially): reaction time, agility, shot analysis, tactics against a weaker side, scoring systems.
