# Package: pe-artistic-aesthetic-movement-s3 — Rhythmic gymnastics

- **loId:** `pe-artistic-aesthetic-movement-s3` · **chapterId:** `pe-artistic-aesthetic-movement`
  · **syllabusRef:** S6
- **Syllabus (skills and techniques):** learners demonstrate proficiency in a short floor
  sequence with balances; pivots/rolls; splits; hops/leaps/skips; body waves/bends/circles/
  swings/turns. Learners set routines to music and use different pieces of apparatus from a
  ball, hoop, ribbon, rope or club.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write in this order: what rhythmic gymnastics
  is; the body movement groups listed (balances, pivots, rolls, splits, hops/leaps/skips, body
  waves/bends/circles/swings/turns) with key points; the five pieces of apparatus with typical
  handling skills; working with music; composing the routine; judging; demands and safety.

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] R1 Rhythmic gymnastics: a floor discipline combining dance-like body movement with handling a piece of apparatus, performed to music.
- [ ] M1 Balances: on one foot (relevé, on the toes) with the free leg held in a clear shape, held still.
- [ ] M2 Pivots: turns on one foot on the ball of the foot, with a fixed body shape.
- [ ] M3 Rolls: controlled body rolls on the floor and rolling the apparatus along the body or floor.
- [ ] M4 Splits: front and side splits on the floor or in the air.
- [ ] M5 Hops, leaps and skips: travelling movements with a clear shape in the air (e.g. split leap with legs at maximum range) and soft landings.
- [ ] M6 Body waves, bends, circles, swings and turns: flowing movements through the whole body, showing flexibility and fluency.
- [ ] A1 Ball: rolls along the arms and floor, bounces, throws and catches with one hand without gripping.
- [ ] A2 Hoop: rolls, rotations around the hand or body, throws and catches, passing through the hoop.
- [ ] A3 Ribbon: continuous patterns (spirals, snakes, circles) with no knots or stopping; the ribbon must stay in motion.
- [ ] A4 Rope: skips and jumps through the rope, swings, circles, throws and catches.
- [ ] A5 Clubs: mills (small circles), swings, tapping, throws and catches of one or both clubs.
- [ ] U1 Music: movements matched to the tempo, phrasing and accents; the routine reflects the mood of the music.
- [ ] C1 Composing the routine: start and end positions; variety of body groups and apparatus elements; use of space and levels; links that flow; apparatus and body work coordinated.
- [ ] J1 Judging: difficulty (body and apparatus elements); execution (deductions for loss of balance, dropped apparatus, poor technique); artistry (music, expression, composition).
- [ ] S1 Safety: warm-up and flexibility preparation, adequate space between performers, suitable floor, checking apparatus condition.
- [ ] D1 Demands: flexibility, balance, co-ordination (hand–eye with apparatus), muscular endurance, rhythm and timing, concentration.

## 3. Traps

- A ribbon must keep moving; stopping or knotting is a fault.
- Artistic gymnastics is in its own notes; do not mix in vaults or tumbling.

## 4. What exams ask

- Rhythmic gymnastics is used as an aesthetic activity in written answers (aesthetic criteria, music and choreography).

## 5. Practice questions to write

1. 8 marks, Higher: "Describe how a rhythmic gymnast combines body movement and apparatus handling in a routine set to music."
2. 6 marks, Ordinary: "Name three pieces of rhythmic gymnastics apparatus and one skill performed with each."
