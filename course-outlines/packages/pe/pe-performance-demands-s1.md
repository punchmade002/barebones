## 1. Must cover

- [ ] F1 Health: the WHO definition (section 2). Health is broader than fitness.
- [ ] F2 Physical fitness: the ability to carry out daily tasks and physical activity with vigour and without undue fatigue, with energy left to meet emergencies (assumed working definition).
- [ ] F3 The four demands of performance named by the specification: health-related fitness, performance-related fitness, psychological preparation, diet and nutrition. Each has its own subsection in this chapter.
- [ ] F4 Health-related fitness (HRF): the components linked to good health and lower disease risk that everyone needs for daily life: cardio-respiratory endurance, muscular endurance, strength, flexibility, body composition.
- [ ] F5 Performance-related fitness (PRF), also called skill-related fitness: the components needed for successful sporting performance: agility, balance, co-ordination, power, speed, reaction time.
- [ ] F6 Differences: purpose (health and daily living versus sporting performance); who needs them (everyone versus performers in a given activity); trainability (HRF responds well to training; PRF is more strongly influenced by genetics and is more activity-specific); how they are developed (general exercise versus sport-specific drills and practice).
- [ ] F7 The two overlap: a performer needs both, and many sports need high levels of an HRF component (e.g. cardio-respiratory endurance in Gaelic football). Evaluate the statement "health-related fitness is the same as performance-related fitness" (a 2022 Higher question): inaccurate, for the reasons in F6, but they are linked.
- [ ] F8 Classifying a given component as HRF or PRF (e.g. co-ordination is PRF; muscular endurance is HRF).

## 3. Traps

- Strength is listed by the specification under health-related fitness; power is performance-related. Do not move them.
- Definitions of each component belong in the next two subsections. Here, list them only.
- Do not use past-paper test results (e.g. a named 16-year-old's scores).

## 4. What exams ask

- Discuss the accuracy of "Health-related fitness is the same as performance-related fitness".
- Discuss the difference between health-related and performance-related fitness (Ordinary, after naming three components).
- Classify named components as HRF or PRF, sometimes in a table with test results.
