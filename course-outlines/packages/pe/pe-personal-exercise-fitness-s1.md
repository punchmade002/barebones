# Package: pe-personal-exercise-fitness-s1 — The personal exercise and fitness area

- **loId:** `pe-personal-exercise-fitness-s1` · **chapterId:** `pe-personal-exercise-fitness` ·
  **syllabusRef:** S6
- **Syllabus:** "Active and purposeful participation in a personally-designed exercise and
  fitness programme that reflect personal interests, goals and principles of training."
  Learners may choose one method of aerobic training and one other conditioning and resistance
  activity. The performance should include: a five-minute warm-up including the necessary
  elements of a warm-up; a fifteen-minute development section with a cardiovascular section and
  a muscular strength and endurance section (which could use a circuit format); a five-minute
  cool-down including the necessary elements of a cool-down. Learners demonstrate knowledge and
  understanding of relevant progressions, adaptations and safe practice. NCCA clarification on
  curriculumonline.ie: training may be aerobic and/or anaerobic.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write in this order: what the area is; the
  choice (one aerobic method plus one conditioning and resistance activity); the 5–15–5
  performance structure with the content of each part; designing the programme for either
  performance or health-related fitness; progressions, adaptations and safe practice; the NCCA
  clarification. Methods and principles have their own subsections.

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] A1 Definition (paraphrase): taking part in a personally designed exercise and fitness programme that reflects the performer's interests, goals and the principles of training.
- [ ] A2 Choice: one aerobic training method (continuous, interval, fartlek, aerobics/step aerobics, spinning, indoor rowing) plus one conditioning and resistance activity (weight training, core stability, circuit training).
- [ ] A3 Goal: the programme aims to improve either performance in a physical activity or health-related fitness.
- [ ] P1 Warm-up (five minutes): pulse raiser, mobility and dynamic stretches, and movements that rehearse the development section.
- [ ] P2 Development section (fifteen minutes): a cardiovascular section and a muscular strength and endurance section; may be organised as a circuit.
- [ ] P3 Cool-down (five minutes): gradual reduction in intensity, then static stretches of the muscles used.
- [ ] D1 Designing the programme: set a goal, choose the methods, apply FITT and the principles of training, choose exercises, set work and recovery, plan progression over weeks, record sessions.
- [ ] Q1 Progressions: increasing load, repetitions, sets, duration or complexity, or reducing recovery, in small steps.
- [ ] Q2 Adaptations: easier or harder versions of each exercise (e.g. a press-up on the knees, against a wall, or with feet raised) so the programme suits the individual.
- [ ] Q3 Safe practice: correct technique and posture, appropriate loads, spotting for heavy lifts, checking equipment, hydration, stopping with pain or dizziness, adequate space.
- [ ] N1 NCCA clarification: the training may be aerobic and/or anaerobic.

## 3. Traps

- Do not re-teach FITT or the principles of training in full; they are in "Applying training principles to each method" and in the chapter on physical and psychological demands.
- Do not give coursework instructions beyond the specification's performance structure.

## 4. What exams ask

- Personal exercise is a context for questions on training methods, principles of training, FITT, warm-up and cool-down, and progression.

## 5. Practice questions to write

1. 8 marks, Higher: "Outline the structure of a personal exercise and fitness performance and describe the content of each part."
2. 6 marks, Ordinary: "Give two progressions and two adaptations for a press-up."
