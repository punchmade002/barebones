## 1. Must cover

- [ ] GF Gaelic football: catch high with hands in a W shape and fingers spread; hand pass with the ball held in one hand and struck with the fist or open palm (not thrown); solo (drop the ball onto the foot and kick it back to the hands while running), toe-to-hand pick-up; kicking from the hands (eyes on the ball, non-kicking foot beside the ball's line, follow through towards target); shoulder-to-shoulder tackle and blocking; key rules: maximum of four steps holding the ball before it must be soloed, bounced or released; the ball may not be bounced twice in a row (soloing may be repeated); no picking the ball directly off the ground with the hands (use the toe-lift).
- [ ] HC Hurling/camogie: ready (dominant hand at the top of the hurley), roll lift and jab lift to pick up; catch with the hand protected; solo with the ball balanced on the bas; striking from the hand and off the ground with hands together for power or apart for control; hook and block to tackle; hand pass (struck with the palm); key rules: the ball may be carried in the hand for a maximum of four steps; it may not be picked directly off the ground with the hand (use a lift).
- [ ] RU Rugby union: pass backwards or level only, hands up as a target, catch with fingers spread, spin pass for distance; tackle: cheek to cheek, shoulder into the thigh, wrap the arms, drive and finish on top; maul and ruck (ball carrier stays on feet in a maul; in a ruck the ball is on the ground and players bind and stay on their feet); side-step and swerve; key rules: forward pass and knock-on are infringements, tackles must be below the shoulders, the tackler must release.
- [ ] BB Basketball: chest pass (thumbs down at release, step into the pass), bounce pass (two-thirds of the way to the receiver), overhead pass; dribble with the fingertips, ball below the waist, eyes up, protect with the body; lay-up (right-hand lay-up off the left foot, two steps); set and jump shot (BEEF: balance, eyes on the target, elbow under the ball, follow through); rebounding (box out); defensive stance (low, feet shoulder-width, slide without crossing feet); pivot footwork; key rules: travelling (moving the pivot foot or too many steps without dribbling), double dribble (two hands, or restarting a stopped dribble), personal fouls for illegal contact, three seconds in the key for attackers.
- [ ] SO Soccer outfield: inside-of-the-foot pass (ankle locked, standing foot beside the ball, follow through to target), lofted pass and chip; cushioning control; dribbling with both feet and changes of pace; shooting with the laces (head over the ball, non-kicking foot beside it); heading with the forehead, eyes open; jockeying (stay goal-side, side-on, delay); tackles (block tackle); key rules: only the goalkeeper handles, in their own penalty area; offside (in the opponents' half, nearer the goal line than the ball and the second-last defender when the ball is played); fouls give free kicks or penalties.
- [ ] SG Soccer goalkeeping: set position; W-shaped catch for high balls and scoop for low balls; diving with the body behind the ball; narrowing the angle; punching crosses; distribution by throw, roll and kick.
- [ ] NB Netball: chest, shoulder, bounce and lob passes; footwork (land on one foot, pivot, no stepping with the landing foot); shooting with the ball on the fingertips above the head, knees bent, flick of the wrist; dodging to get free; marking with arms out at 0.9 m from the player; key rules: three seconds with the ball, no contact, playing areas by position.
- [ ] HO Hockey outfield: grip (left hand at the top, right hand lower down); push pass, slap and hit; stopping the ball on the open stick; dribbling with open and reverse stick; flicks; shooting inside the circle; jockeying and channelling; key rules: only the flat side of the stick may be used, goals only from inside the shooting circle, no dangerous play with the ball or stick.
- [ ] HG Hockey goalkeeping: protective kit; kicking clearances; saving with pads and kickers; defending short corners; facing penalty flicks; diving and sliding saves.
- [ ] HB Olympic handball: catch with hands forming a W; one-handed passes; three steps with the ball and three seconds; jump shot from outside the goal area; penalty throw; moving free; defensive marking in a zone; key rules: only the goalkeeper may stand in the goal area, maximum three steps and three seconds.

## 3. Traps

- Rugby passes go backwards or level, never forwards.
- In hockey only the flat (face) side of the stick is used.
- Keep each game to its listed skill families; do not add game histories.

## 4. What exams ask

- Invasion games are the most common context: Gaelic football, hurling, basketball and soccer appear repeatedly (skills, strategies such as the pick and roll, officiating and scoring, fitness components, energy systems).
