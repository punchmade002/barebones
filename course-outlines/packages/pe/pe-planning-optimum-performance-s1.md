# Package: pe-planning-optimum-performance-s1 — Personal performance analysis

- **loId:** `pe-planning-optimum-performance-s1` · **chapterId:**
  `pe-planning-optimum-performance` · **syllabusRef:** 4.1
- **Syllabus:** "examine the different factors that influence personal performance in physical
  activity and sport". The topic introduction describes the planning cycle: analyse performance,
  set realistic goals from sound theory, design a training/practice programme, monitor and adapt
  it, then evaluate a concluding performance with the same methods. Ordinary and Higher level;
  no Higher Level only material.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write in this order: what performance analysis
  is and why it is done; the planning cycle (overview only); the factors that influence
  performance, grouped; choosing and justifying the most important factors for a named activity.

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] A1 Performance analysis: the systematic observation, measurement and evaluation of a performance (or part of it) to identify strengths and weaknesses and inform improvement.
- [ ] A2 Benefits to the performer: objective evidence instead of impressions; identifies priorities; sets realistic goals; tracks progress; motivates; improves decision-making and technique.
- [ ] A3 The planning cycle in four stages: analyse → set goals → train/practise with ongoing monitoring and reflection → concluding analysis with the same methods. The next three subsections cover each stage in detail.

Factors that influence personal performance (each: how it affects performance, with an example)
- [ ] F1 Physical: fitness levels (HRF and PRF), health, injury, body type, age and maturation, fatigue.
- [ ] F2 Technical: level of skill and technique, consistency.
- [ ] F3 Tactical: game understanding, decision-making, knowledge of structures and strategies.
- [ ] F4 Psychological: confidence, anxiety and arousal, motivation, concentration.
- [ ] F5 Lifestyle: diet and hydration, sleep, alcohol and smoking, balancing study, work and training.
- [ ] F6 Social: family support, peers and team-mates, coach relationship, role models.
- [ ] F7 Environmental and facilities: weather and playing surface, quality of facilities and equipment, access to coaching, home or away venue, altitude and heat.
- [ ] F8 Economic: cost of equipment, membership, travel and coaching.
- [ ] F9 Choosing the two most important factors for a named activity and justifying the choice (e.g. for a basketball free-throw shooter: technique and concentration; for a marathon runner: cardio-respiratory endurance and pacing/motivation).
