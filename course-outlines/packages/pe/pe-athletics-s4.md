# Package: pe-athletics-s4 — Jumping events

- **loId:** `pe-athletics-s4` · **chapterId:** `pe-athletics` · **syllabusRef:** S6
- **Syllabus:** jumping events high jump, long jump, triple jump; skills and techniques: run
  up; take off; flight; landing; overall efficiency.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write in this order: principles (horizontal
  speed converted to height or distance, take-off angle, centre of mass); then one block per
  event with the same headings (run-up, take-off, flight, landing); rules and fouls; overall
  efficiency and common faults; safety.

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] P1 Jumping converts the speed of the run-up into vertical (high jump) or horizontal (long and triple jump) distance at take-off; distance depends on take-off speed, angle and height of the centre of mass.
- [ ] P2 Take-off uses a powerful, extended drive (hip, knee and ankle) and the free leg and arms swing upward to add lift; Newton's third law (ground reaction force).
- [ ] HJ High jump (Fosbury flop): curved (J-shaped) approach, straight then curving in the final strides; lean away from the bar on the curve; take off from the foot further from the bar, driving the knee of the free leg up; rotate so the back faces the bar; arch over the bar with the head and shoulders back and hips high, then lift the legs; land on the upper back and shoulders on the mat.
- [ ] LJ Long jump: accelerating run-up with a consistent number of strides (checked by a check mark); flat-footed, active take-off from the board without stopping; drive the free knee up; flight technique (hang, sail or hitch-kick) to keep balance and delay the legs dropping; landing with legs extended forward, then fall forward or sideways (not back).
- [ ] TJ Triple jump: run-up as for long jump; hop (take off and land on the same foot), step (land on the other foot), jump (land in the pit on both feet); keep the phases balanced (roughly even rhythm) and maintain speed; active, flat-footed landings; arm action (single or double arm) to drive each phase.
- [ ] R1 Rules and fouls: high jump (one-foot take-off, three consecutive failures eliminate the jumper, the bar must stay on); long and triple jump (stepping over the take-off board line is a foul, measured from the board to the nearest mark in the sand, wind limits for records).
- [ ] O1 Overall efficiency: consistent run-up, speed maintained through take-off, upright posture at take-off, controlled landing.
- [ ] O2 Common faults: slowing before the board, overstriding at take-off, sitting back on landing, flattening the curve in high jump.
- [ ] S1 Safety: pits dug and raked, landing mats positioned and checked, runway clear, spikes checked, take-off area dry.

## 3. Traps

- The high jumper takes off from the foot further from the bar.
- The triple jump sequence is hop (same foot), step (other foot), jump.

## 4. What exams ask

- Jumps are contexts for power, planes and axes, Newton's laws, energy systems (long jump: ATP-PC), and fitness testing.
