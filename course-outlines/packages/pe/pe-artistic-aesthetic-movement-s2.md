# Package: pe-artistic-aesthetic-movement-s2 — Artistic gymnastics

- **loId:** `pe-artistic-aesthetic-movement-s2` · **chapterId:** `pe-artistic-aesthetic-movement`
  · **syllabusRef:** S6
- **Syllabus (skills and techniques):** jumps (minimum 2): tuck/pike/straddle/180-degree turn;
  rolls (minimum 2): forward/backwards/other; balances (minimum 2):
  handstand/headstand/arabesque/other; weight transference (minimum 2): cartwheel/round
  off/hand spring/back flip. At least four of these agilities in the short floor sequence:
  backward roll to handstand, handspring, back flip, front somersault, handstand ½ turn,
  one-handed cartwheel, back somersault, back/forward walkovers. Proficiency in at least two
  vaults from: handspring, short arm overswing, optional vault of similar tariff (not through or
  straddle).
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write in this order: the requirements (minimums);
  jumps; rolls; balances; weight transference; the agilities for the floor sequence; vaults;
  building the floor sequence (linking, compositional elements); judging criteria; safety and
  spotting; demands. Each skill: key technique points (two to four).

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] Q1 Requirements summary: at least 2 jumps, 2 rolls, 2 balances, 2 weight transferences; at least 4 listed agilities in the short floor sequence; at least 2 vaults from the list.
- [ ] J1 Jumps: take-off from two feet with arm swing; tuck (knees to chest), pike (legs straight, together, forward), straddle (legs straight and apart), 180-degree turn; controlled two-footed landing, knees bent, arms out.
- [ ] R1 Forward roll: tuck the chin, hands flat, push with legs, roll on the rounded back, come up onto the feet without hands.
- [ ] R2 Backward roll: tuck tight, hands by the ears with palms up, roll back, push through the hands to land on the feet.
- [ ] B1 Handstand: straight arms shoulder-width, kick up from a lunge, body straight with tight core, shoulders over hands, held still, step down.
- [ ] B2 Headstand: triangle base (forehead and two hands), hips over the head, legs raised with control.
- [ ] B3 Arabesque: balance on one straight leg, the other leg extended behind, arms out, chest lifted.
- [ ] W1 Cartwheel: lunge, hands placed one after the other in a line, legs pass straight over the top in the frontal plane, land one foot after the other facing sideways.
- [ ] W2 Round-off: like a cartwheel with a quarter turn, legs snap together overhead and land on two feet facing back the way you came; links into backward skills.
- [ ] W3 Handspring: run and hurdle, hands placed, legs swing straight over, push from the shoulders, land on the feet with arms up.
- [ ] W4 Back flip (back handspring): sit back as if into a chair, swing arms back and up, jump backwards onto the hands, snap the legs over to land.
- [ ] G1 The other listed agilities in one line each: backward roll to handstand; front somersault (rotation without hands); handstand with half turn; one-handed cartwheel; back somersault; back and forward walkovers.
- [ ] V1 Handspring vault: run, two-foot take-off from the springboard, hands on the vault table with straight arms, body rotates forward over the hands, push off, land facing away.
- [ ] V2 Short arm overswing: name it as one of the specified vaults only; no checked technique description is available, so give none.
- [ ] V3 Optional vault of similar difficulty, excluding through and straddle vaults.
- [ ] F1 Building the floor sequence: a planned start and finish, smooth linking movements (steps, turns, jumps), variety of levels and directions, use of the floor area, contrasting speed, controlled landings.
- [ ] C1 Judging: difficulty of the elements; execution (deductions for bent legs, flexed feet, loss of balance, poor landings); artistry and composition.
- [ ] S1 Safety and spotting: warm-up and conditioning, progressions (e.g. handstand against a wall before free handstand), mats placed correctly, trained spotters supporting at the hips and shoulders, checking springboard and vault, no jewellery.
- [ ] D1 Demands: flexibility, strength (upper body and core), power, balance, co-ordination, spatial awareness, courage and concentration.
