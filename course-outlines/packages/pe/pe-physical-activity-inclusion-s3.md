# Package: pe-physical-activity-inclusion-s3 — Developments over the past twenty years

- **loId:** `pe-physical-activity-inclusion-s3` · **chapterId:**
  `pe-physical-activity-inclusion` · **syllabusRef:** 7.3
- **Not prescribed for June 2027.** Topic 7 is not one of the two topics the SEC prescribed
  for the 2027 examination (circular S51/25). Write it after the prescribed chapters.
- **Syllabus:** "discuss developments in physical activity and sport over the last twenty
  years from one group's perspective". The outline asks for one block per group, so students
  can choose. Ordinary and Higher level; no Higher Level only material.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write one block per group (women, older adults,
  people with physical disability, people with intellectual disability, different ethnic groups,
  different socio-economic groups), each with two or three developments, their impact on that
  group, and what still needs to change. Use only the checked developments in section 2; where a
  group has few checked named developments, describe the type of development without naming
  one.

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] D1 Women: increased visibility and coverage (the 20x20 campaign; televised women's Gaelic games, rugby and soccer); equal match payments for Ireland's senior women's and men's international soccer players; Olympic success (women's boxing added at the 2012 Olympics, where Katie Taylor won gold); policy (Sport Ireland's Women in Sport policy and funding); more female coaches and administrators.
- [ ] D2 Older adults: Go for Life programmes; growth of walking groups, parkrun and masters competitions; age-friendly gym classes and "get active" initiatives.
- [ ] D3 People with physical disability: growth and coverage of the Paralympic Games and Irish success there; Sports Inclusion Disability Officers in Local Sports Partnerships; more accessible facilities; wheelchair and adapted versions of mainstream sports.
- [ ] D4 People with intellectual disability: Special Olympics World Summer Games held in Dublin in 2003; growth of Special Olympics clubs and inclusive club sessions; unified sports.
- [ ] D5 Different ethnic groups: growth of a more diverse population playing Irish and international sports; anti-racism education and campaigns by NGBs and SARI; inclusive dress rules.
- [ ] D6 Different socio-economic groups: free mass-participation events such as parkrun; Local Sports Partnership programmes in disadvantaged areas; outdoor gyms, greenways and public facilities.
- [ ] I1 For each development: the impact on participation, role models, attitudes and opportunities for that group.
- [ ] R1 For each group: what still needs to change.

## 3. Traps

- The syllabus asks for developments "over the last twenty years"; avoid developments before about 2005 except the 2003 Games as context.
- Do not invent participation statistics or funding amounts.

## 4. What exams ask

- Describe how two developments over the past twenty years have enhanced opportunities for one group.
- Discuss two developments since 2005 for one group.
- Identify three developments on a timeline for one group and describe one and its impact (Ordinary).

## 5. Practice questions to write

1. 10 marks, Higher: "Discuss two developments in physical activity and sport over the past twenty years for one group of your choice and their impact."
2. 6 marks, Ordinary: "Describe one development in sport over the past twenty years for women and outline its impact."
