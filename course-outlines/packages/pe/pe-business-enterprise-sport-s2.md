# Package: pe-business-enterprise-sport-s2 — The business dimension

- **loId:** `pe-business-enterprise-sport-s2` · **chapterId:** `pe-business-enterprise-sport` ·
  **syllabusRef:** 10.2
- **Prescribed for June 2027** (SEC circular S51/25). Write in full Higher Level depth.
- **Syllabus:** "investigate the involvement of a selected business in sport". The topic
  introduction: physical activity and sport are big businesses; powerful groups compete for huge
  audiences and sport-generated wealth and control what we see on screen. Ordinary and Higher
  level; no Higher Level only material.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write in this order: the sport industry (sectors
  and revenue streams); how to investigate a business; two worked investigations (an Irish
  sportswear manufacturer and a sports stadium/venue business); evaluating a business's
  involvement. Exams ask "outline three key findings from your investigation", so each worked
  investigation must end in clear findings.

## 1. Must cover

The sport industry
- [ ] B1 Sectors: sportswear and equipment manufacturers and retailers; media and broadcasters; stadiums and venues; gyms and leisure centres; sports nutrition; event organisers; tourism and travel; betting companies; sports clubs and NGBs as businesses.
- [ ] B2 Revenue streams in sport: broadcasting rights, sponsorship, ticketing and hospitality, merchandising, membership and participation fees, facility hire, events.
- [ ] B3 Enterprise: identifying a need in sport and meeting it with a product, service or event (e.g. a coaching camp, a sports app, a fitness class); target market, value, costs, revenue, risk.

How to investigate a business
- [ ] V1 Questions: what it sells and to whom; how it is involved in sport (supplier, sponsor, owner, broadcaster); how it earns money from sport; how it promotes itself through sport; its impact on participation, the sport and the local economy; ethical issues; challenges.
- [ ] V2 Sources: the company's website and annual reports, news articles, interviews, visiting the business, its social media.

Worked investigation 1: O'Neills (Irish sportswear manufacturer)
- [ ] O1 What it is: an Irish manufacturer of sportswear, best known for GAA jerseys and team kit, also supplying rugby, soccer and other club and school kits.
- [ ] O2 Involvement: supplies kit to county and club teams and schools; kit deals give it visibility; sells replica jerseys and leisurewear to supporters; sponsors and supports clubs and events.
- [ ] O3 Findings to present: its business depends on the club and county calendar and on GAA culture; it employs people in manufacturing in Ireland; replica jerseys turn county identity into sales; it competes with large international brands.

Worked investigation 2: Croke Park as a business
- [ ] K1 What it is: the GAA's headquarters and stadium in Dublin, used for major GAA games.
- [ ] K2 Business activities: ticketing and corporate hospitality for games; concerts and other events; conference and meeting facilities; the GAA Museum and stadium tours; naming and advertising opportunities.
- [ ] K3 Findings to present: non-GAA events earn income that supports the GAA; the stadium brings visitors and spending to the surrounding area; the GAA is an amateur organisation whose players are not paid, while the stadium operates commercially; local residents can be affected by large events.

Evaluating a business's involvement
- [ ] E1 Benefits to sport: funding, equipment, jobs, publicity, facilities.
- [ ] E2 Concerns: commercial interests may outweigh participants' interests, costs to families (replica kits, tickets), unhealthy or gambling products, influence over scheduling, exploitation of athletes' images.

## 3. Traps

- Do not copy the past-paper examples (a sports retailer, a nutrition company) word for word; use the checked businesses here.

## 4. What exams ask

- Discuss the involvement of a named business in sport (Ordinary).
- Outline three key findings from your investigation of a named business; analyse the involvement of a named business in sport (Higher).
