# Package: pe-athletics-s1 — The athletics area

- **loId:** `pe-athletics-s1` · **chapterId:** `pe-athletics` · **syllabusRef:** S6
- **Syllabus:** "Athletics—running, jumping, throwing and walking events performed indoors or
  outdoors which are measurable in terms of the performer's own ability but also against
  standards set by others' achievements." Learners select one category (running, throwing or
  jumping); the performance assessment focuses on one activity within it (e.g. discus).
  Section 7: the performance assessment covers the listed skills, tactics, and rules and safe
  practice.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. A short overview. Write in this order: what the
  area is; the three categories and their events; the choice rule; measurement (time and
  distance) and why it suits performance analysis; general rules and officials; safety common
  to athletics; the performance assessment; using athletics as the named activity.

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] A1 Definition (paraphrase): running, jumping, throwing and walking events, indoors or outdoors, measured against the performer's own standard and against others.
- [ ] A2 Categories and events: running (sprints, middle distance, long distance, hurdles); throwing (shot-put, discus, javelin, hammer); jumping (high jump, long jump, triple jump).
- [ ] A3 Choice rule: one category, and one event within it for the performance assessment.
- [ ] M1 Measurement: times and distances are objective, so improvement is easy to record and compare (personal bests, season's bests), which suits goal setting and performance analysis.
- [ ] R1 General rules and officials: starter and false starts, lane rules, track judges, field judges who measure and check fouls, timekeepers or electronic timing, numbers of attempts in field events.
- [ ] S1 Safety: warm-up; throwing areas cleared and controlled (throw only on command, retrieve only on command, never walk across a throwing sector); cages for discus and hammer; landing pits raked and mats in place; spikes checked; implements of the correct weight for age.
- [ ] P1 Performance assessment: one event; the listed skills and techniques; the rules, conventions and safe practice of the event.
- [ ] W1 Athletics as a named activity in written answers: energy systems (100 m vs 400 m vs 10,000 m), levers and Newton's laws (throws and jumps), planes and axes (discus and hammer rotation), fitness components and testing, periodisation.

## 3. Traps

- Walking events are named in the definition but not in the specification's activity tables; mention them only in the definition.
- Do not teach event technique here; each category has its own notes.

## 4. What exams ask

- Athletics is a frequent context in past papers: a sprinter's practice principles, the discus and hammer planes and axes, the javelin's lever and practice method, component of fitness for 10,000 m versus shot-put, energy systems in running events.
