# Package: pe-aquatics-s5 — Water polo

- **loId:** `pe-aquatics-s5` · **chapterId:** `pe-aquatics` · **syllabusRef:** S6
- **Syllabus (skills and techniques):** passing/receiving skills (various); shooting skills
  (various); moving with the ball; moving free; marking/intercepting skills.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write in this order: the game in outline;
  basic movement (eggbeater kick, head-up front crawl); each listed skill family with key
  points; basic rules and fouls; positions and simple tactics; safety; demands.

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] G1 The game: two teams of seven (six field players and a goalkeeper) try to throw the ball into the opponents' goal; players may not touch the bottom (except the goalkeeper in some pools), and may handle the ball with only one hand (the goalkeeper may use two).
- [ ] B1 Eggbeater kick: alternating circular leg kick that keeps the player high and stable in the water to pass, shoot and defend.
- [ ] B2 Head-up front crawl: to see the ball and players while swimming.
- [ ] P1 Passing and receiving: dry pass (thrown to the receiver's hand above the water, caught without touching the water) and wet pass (into the water in front of a team-mate who swims onto it); use the eggbeater to rise; pass with one hand from above the head.
- [ ] S1 Shooting: power shot from above the head with eggbeater lift and body rotation; push shot from the water surface; backhand; lob over the goalkeeper; aim for the corners.
- [ ] M1 Moving with the ball (dribbling): swimming head-up front crawl with the ball between the arms, pushed forward by the bow wave.
- [ ] F1 Moving free: sprinting and changing direction to lose a marker; driving towards goal; positioning to receive.
- [ ] D1 Marking and intercepting: staying between the opponent and the goal, using the eggbeater to stay high, arm up to block passes and shots, anticipating passes.
- [ ] R1 Basic rules: one hand only on the ball (field players); no holding, sinking or pulling an opponent who does not have the ball (ordinary or exclusion fouls); a shot clock limits possession; the 2 m and 5 m lines.
- [ ] T1 Positions and tactics: centre forward (hole set) positioned in front of goal; drivers; counter-attack after a turnover; zone or man-to-man defence; exploiting an extra player after an exclusion.
- [ ] Z1 Safety: caps with ear guards; trimmed fingernails; no jewellery; supervised deep water.
- [ ] Y1 Demands: cardio-respiratory and muscular endurance (legs for the eggbeater), power for shooting, agility in the water, physical contest.
