# Package: pe-planning-optimum-performance-s2 — Methods of analysis

- **loId:** `pe-planning-optimum-performance-s2` · **chapterId:**
  `pe-planning-optimum-performance` · **syllabusRef:** 4.2
- **Syllabus:** "identify appropriate methods of analysing different aspects of performance:
  skill and technique; structures and strategies; choreography; performance-related fitness;
  psychological preparedness"; "use a selection of tools, including video and analysis software
  to analyse their own and others' performances"; "compare their personal performance to that
  of a more skilled/model performer"; "identify four areas from their performance which require
  further development". Ordinary and Higher level; no Higher Level only material.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write in this order: the main tools; a method
  for each of the five aspects (plus health-related fitness, which exams also ask); video and
  analysis software; comparing with a model performer; identifying four areas for development.
  Exams require a *different* method for each aspect, so give at least two per aspect.

## 1. Must cover

Tools (each: what it is, what it shows, strengths and limitations)
- [ ] T1 Observation with a checklist or observation schedule built from key coaching points.
- [ ] T2 Video analysis: recording and reviewing performance, slow motion, frame-by-frame, freeze-frame, side-by-side and overlay comparison, drawing tools in analysis software or apps.
- [ ] T3 Notational analysis: tallying events during performance (e.g. passes completed, turnovers, shots and scores, unforced errors) to produce statistics; can be done by hand or with software.
- [ ] T4 Fitness test batteries and fitness data (heart-rate monitors, GPS units for distance and speed).
- [ ] T5 Questionnaires and self-report inventories (e.g. a competitive anxiety questionnaire such as SCAT, a mood profile), interviews, reflective diaries.
- [ ] T6 Judges' criteria and scoring sheets for judged activities.

A method for each aspect
- [ ] M1 Skill and technique: video with a technique checklist of key coaching points; comparison with a model performer; success rate out of a set number of attempts.
- [ ] M2 Structures and strategies: notational analysis of team actions (e.g. where possession is lost, success of set plays); match video with positional tagging.
- [ ] M3 Choreography: video reviewed against a checklist of compositional requirements (use of space, levels, variety, musicality, transitions); judges' or peers' scoring against criteria.
- [ ] M4 Performance-related fitness: a test battery (e.g. 30 m sprint, Illinois agility, vertical jump, ruler drop).
- [ ] M5 Health-related fitness: a test battery (e.g. multistage fitness test, sit-and-reach, press-up test).
- [ ] M6 Psychological preparedness: a validated questionnaire before competition (e.g. SCAT for anxiety), interviews, a reflective diary rating confidence and concentration.

Comparing with a model performer
- [ ] C1 How: choose a more skilled performer at the same skill; record both from the same angle; compare phase by phase against key coaching points; note differences.
- [ ] C2 Why it helps: gives a clear visual target, identifies specific technical faults, motivates, helps set realistic goals.
- [ ] C3 Limitations: the model's body type, strength and experience differ; copying a style is not always appropriate.

Four areas for development
- [ ] F1 From the analysis, identify four specific areas (e.g. one technical, one tactical, one fitness, one psychological), each supported by evidence from the analysis data. These become the four performance goals in the next stage.

## 3. Traps

- Use a different method for each aspect when asked; do not answer "video analysis" for every aspect.
- Fitness tests themselves are taught in `pe-performance-demands` ("Assessing fitness"); here name tests only as methods.
- Aesthetic criteria are the next subsection.

## 4. What exams ask

- Outline an appropriate method of analysing each of choreography, psychological preparedness, skill and technique, and structures and strategies, without repeating a method.
- Identify methods to analyse health-related fitness and psychological preparedness, and explain how you would use one.
- Outline two reasons why comparing your performance with a more skilled performer helps; describe how you conducted such an analysis.
- Identify two methods of analysing skill and technique and two tests of fitness for an athlete.

## 5. Practice questions to write

1. 8 marks, Higher: "Outline an appropriate method of analysing each of: choreography; psychological preparedness; skill and technique; structures and strategies. Use a different method for each." Model: four distinct methods with one line on how each is used.
2. 8 marks, Higher: "Describe how you would compare your personal performance to that of a model performer and outline two benefits of doing so."
3. 6 marks, Ordinary: "Identify a method to analyse health-related fitness and explain how you would use it."
