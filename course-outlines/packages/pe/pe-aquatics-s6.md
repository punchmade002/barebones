# Package: pe-aquatics-s6 — Synchronised swimming

- **loId:** `pe-aquatics-s6` · **chapterId:** `pe-aquatics` · **syllabusRef:** S6
- **Syllabus (skills and techniques):** back layout, tuck position; front layout, front pike;
  back pike, ballet leg; fishtail, flamingo and bent-knee vertical; dolphin arch, surface arch,
  split walk over, front; duet or trio performance including combinations of these and other
  synchronised swimming movements.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. Write in this order: what synchronised
  (artistic) swimming is; sculling as the foundation; the basic body positions listed (one
  bullet each describing the shape); figures and transitions; the duet or trio routine
  (choreography, synchronisation, use of music, artistic impression); judging criteria;
  demands and safety.

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] A1 Synchronised swimming (now called artistic swimming): performing figures and routines to music, solo or in a group, judged on execution and artistic impression; Swim Ireland governs it in Ireland.
- [ ] S1 Sculling: continuous figure-of-eight hand movements that support and move the body; standard scull, support scull (in vertical positions), propelling scull.
- [ ] P1 Back layout: floating on the back, body straight and horizontal, face, chest, thighs and feet at the surface.
- [ ] P2 Front layout: face down, body straight and horizontal at the surface.
- [ ] P3 Tuck position: knees and hips bent and drawn to the chest, body compact.
- [ ] P4 Front pike: bent at the hips at 90 degrees, legs straight and together, trunk straight; used as the starting shape for many figures.
- [ ] P5 Back pike: bent at the hips at about 90 degrees with legs straight and together, facing upwards.
- [ ] P6 Ballet leg: from back layout, one leg raised vertically, straight, while the other stays at the surface.
- [ ] P7 Vertical (upside-down) positions: body vertical and head-down with legs extended out of the water. Fishtail: one leg vertical, the other extended forward horizontally at the surface. Bent-knee vertical: one leg vertical, the other bent with its toe touching the inside of the straight leg's knee.
- [ ] P7b Flamingo: a back-based position, one leg extended vertically as in ballet leg, the other drawn up towards the chest with the shin parallel to the surface.
- [ ] P8 Arch positions: surface arch (the lower back arched, legs at the surface) and dolphin arch (the body arched as it travels head-first in a circle under the water, as in the dolphin figure).
- [ ] P9 Split walkover front: from a front pike or vertical, legs split and walk over to finish.
- [ ] F1 Figures and transitions: moving smoothly from one position to another with control, height and stability.
- [ ] R1 Duet or trio routine: choreographing a sequence that combines the positions and figures; synchronisation (timing with each other and with the music); patterns and formations; use of the pool space; entries and exits; expression and artistic impression.
- [ ] J1 Judging criteria: execution (precision, height, control, synchronisation) and artistic impression (choreography, musicality, manner of presentation); difficulty.
- [ ] D1 Demands: breath control and cardio-respiratory endurance; flexibility; core and leg strength; co-ordination; spatial awareness under water; concentration and teamwork.
- [ ] Z1 Safety: supervised deep water, gradual breath-hold training without hyperventilation, nose clips.
