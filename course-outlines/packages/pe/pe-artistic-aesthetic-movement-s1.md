# Package: pe-artistic-aesthetic-movement-s1 — The artistic and aesthetic area

- **loId:** `pe-artistic-aesthetic-movement-s1` · **chapterId:** `pe-artistic-aesthetic-movement`
  · **syllabusRef:** S6
- **Syllabus:** "Artistic activities provide learners with opportunities to experience creative
  crafting and performance of activities which involve imaginative combinations of skills,
  techniques and/or movements in the appropriate style of the event. Aesthetic activities
  provide learners with opportunities to demonstrate their appreciation of the qualities of the
  movement and the expressive abilities of the performers. Learners demonstrate this by
  adopting aesthetic criteria in planning for, performing and evaluating the activity."
  Options: gymnastics (artistic, rhythmic); dance (contemporary, folk, modern, ballet, jazz,
  tap, ethnic, traditional). Section 7: the performance assessment covers the listed skills,
  compositional knowledge, and rules and safe practice.
- **Prepared by:** a higher model, 2026-10-30. A short overview. Write in this order: artistic
  versus aesthetic activities (the specification's wording); the options and the choice rule;
  how the area links to compositional elements and to aesthetic and artistic criteria; safe
  practice common to gymnastics and dance; the performance assessment. Do not re-teach the
  criteria or compositional elements; apply them in one or two bullets.

## 1. Must cover

- [ ] A1 Artistic activities: creative crafting and performance of imaginative combinations of skills and movements in the style of the event.
- [ ] A2 Aesthetic activities: appreciating the qualities of movement and the expressiveness of performers, using aesthetic criteria to plan, perform and evaluate.
- [ ] A3 The options: artistic gymnastics, rhythmic gymnastics, and dance in one of contemporary, folk, modern, ballet, jazz, tap, ethnic or traditional styles; one activity from this area may be one of the student's three.
- [ ] L1 Links: routines are built from compositional elements (action, space, dynamics, relationships, choreographic form) and judged against aesthetic and artistic criteria (body line, control, fluency, musicality, expression, originality).
- [ ] S1 Safe practice: thorough warm-up including mobility, correct progressions, spotting and supporting in gymnastics, mats positioned and checked, apparatus checked, suitable clothing (no jewellery, hair tied back), appropriate floor surface for dance, hydration, avoiding overuse.
- [ ] P1 Performance assessment: the listed skills within a routine or dance, compositional and choreographic knowledge, and safe practice; performances captured on video.
