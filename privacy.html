Who is responsible for your data

Gabriel Whelan, operating barebones in Ireland, is the data controller for personal data collected through barebones.ie and the barebones app. Contact: 1stcitizen@barebones.ie.

What we collect

  • Account details: your username, email address and password. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form; we can never see it.
  • Study data: your progress, flashcards you’ve learned, test results, timer use, and the subjects and goals you choose when you start.
  • Feedback: messages you send us through the app, and our replies.
  • Consent records: when you agreed to these policies, which version, and whether you chose to get emails from us.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic device and browser information, which our hosting and delivery providers receive whenever your browser loads a page.

We don’t ask for your real name, school, date of birth, grades or any special category data, and we don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide your account and save your progress: needed to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • To answer feedback and keep barebones secure: our legitimate interest in running a safe, working service.
  • To understand how the site is used: our legitimate interest, using privacy-friendly, aggregated analytics that don’t use cookies or identify you.
  • To send you news and study tips by email: only if you tick the box to agree (consent). You can unsubscribe or withdraw consent at any time.
  • To send essential account emails (confirming your email, password resets, important changes): needed to provide the service.

Students under 16

In Ireland, the age at which you can consent to online services yourself is 16. If you are under 16, a parent or guardian must agree before you create an account. If we learn that an account was created by someone under 16 without that agreement, we will delete it. Parents and guardians can contact us at any time about their child’s account.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: account sign-in and database storage.
  • Vercel: website hosting and cookie-free web analytics and speed measurement.
  • jsDelivr and Google Fonts: deliver code libraries and fonts to your browser, so they receive your IP address.
  • Our email provider: only if you agree to receive emails from us.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where they do, transfers are protected by the EU’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We may also share data if the law requires it.

Cookies and storage on your device

barebones doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. The app saves your sign-in session and study progress in your browser’s local storage so it can work. This is strictly necessary to provide the service, so we don’t ask for consent for it. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings, which will sign you out.

How long we keep it

  • Account and study data: for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, apart from backups, which are overwritten soon after.
  • Feedback messages: for as long as your account exists, or until they’re no longer needed.
  • Accounts unused for 3 years: we may delete them, after emailing you first.
  • Records of your consent: kept while your account exists, so we can show what you agreed to.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your data;
  • correct data that’s wrong;
  • have your data deleted;
  • get your data in a format you can take elsewhere;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent to emails at any time.

Email 1stcitizen@barebones.ie from your account’s email address and we’ll reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Keeping it safe

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and database access rules mean each account can only read its own study data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password you don’t use anywhere else. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change applies. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
