    Seeds

    A seed is a fertilised, mature ovule. It contains an embryo (a new plant in miniature), a food store and a protective coat, the testa. It is the product of sexual reproduction in flowering plants.

    Fruit and seed dispersal

    Dispersal is the carrying of fruits and seeds away from the parent plant. Plants cannot move, so dispersal is how they spread their offspring.
