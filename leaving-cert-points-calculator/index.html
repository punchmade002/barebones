    How CAO points are worked out

    • Best six count. Every subject you sit is scored, but only your six highest results go towards your points total.
    • One sitting. Points come from one Leaving Cert. You can't add a subject from a repeat year to this year's results.
    • Higher Maths bonus. A H6 or better in Higher Level Maths earns 25 extra points, as long as Maths makes your best six.
    • 625 is the maximum. Six H1s give 600, and the Maths bonus takes it to 625.

    The Higher Maths bonus, in one example

    Say your results are H2, H2, H3, H3, H4 and H5, plus a H6 in Higher Maths. On grades alone, Maths would be your lowest result at 46 points and wouldn't count. With the bonus it is worth 71, which beats the H5's 56. So Maths goes into your best six, the H5 drops out, and your total goes from 452 to 467.

    Points aren't the only thing that matters

    Points get you ranked for a course. Separately, each course has minimum entry requirements, such as a certain grade in Maths, Irish or a language, or a set number of Higher Level subjects. If you miss a requirement, the course is off the table whatever your points. Check each course's requirements on cao.ie or the college's own site.

    Points calculator FAQ

    How are Leaving Cert points calculated?

    Each grade is worth a set number of points on the CAO Common Points Scale, from 100 for a H1 down to 0 for a H8 or O7. Only your best six subjects from one sitting of the Leaving Cert count, and a H6 or higher in Higher Level Maths earns 25 bonus points. The maximum is 625.

    What is the maximum number of CAO points?

    625. That is six H1s (600) plus the 25 bonus points for Higher Level Maths.

    Do the 25 bonus points for Higher Maths always count?

    No. You get the bonus for a H6 or higher in Higher Level Maths, but only if Maths, with the bonus added, is one of your best six results. A H6 in Maths is worth 46 + 25 = 71 points.

    Does Foundation Level Maths count for CAO points?

    Only at some colleges. Where it is accepted, an F1 is worth 20 points and an F2 is worth 12. Check the entry requirements of each course you are applying for.

    Does LCVP count for points?

    Yes. The LCVP Link Modules count as one subject: a Distinction is worth 66 points, a Merit 46 and a Pass 28.

    Can I combine results from two Leaving Certs for points?

    No. Points are calculated from one sitting of the Leaving Certificate. Some colleges let you combine sittings to meet minimum entry requirements, but not to add up points.
