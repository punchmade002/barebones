## Pipeline guidance

- Create one scaffold chapter per numbered unit and preserve the topic aliases for tagging.
- Teach a concept as: precise definition, stakeholder/context, applied example, consequence, and
  a short exam-style check.
- For planning, finance and decision topics, include assumptions and a visible method before the
  conclusion.
- Treat the Investigative Study and Written Exam as assessment nodes, not ordinary content
  chapters.
- Generate original explanations and scenarios; do not reproduce SimpleStudy prose or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- Business law, consumer protection and economic policy can change. Verify time-sensitive facts
  against current primary sources before publication.
- The SimpleStudy title “Devloping Questions to Research” contained a spelling error; this guide
  normalises it to “Developing”.
