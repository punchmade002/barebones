# Business worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy commonly uses sectioned explanation, note callouts and short worked applications for
research/decision topics. Follow that presentation rhythm only. Real question wording and reward
points must come from SEC papers and marking schemes; all teaching prose and scenarios must be
original.

## Required answer shape

1. Identify the command word and business concept.
2. State the concept accurately.
3. Apply it to a named fact from the scenario.
4. Explain the business consequence.
5. Conclude or recommend only where the command requires it.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** A small bakery is deciding whether to extend its opening hours. Show how it
could frame a useful research question.

**Model shape:** “Would opening from 7 a.m. to 8 a.m. on weekdays increase average daily sales by
at least 8% over a six-week trial?” The question identifies the decision, period and measurable
success criterion. The bakery could compare weekday sales and costs during the trial with a
representative baseline before deciding whether the extra labour cost is justified.
