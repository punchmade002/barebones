## Pipeline guidance

- Keep performing, composing and listening as separate but linked scaffold dimensions.
- Analytical answers should identify audible/notated evidence, use correct terminology, locate it
  precisely and explain its musical effect.
- Composition models must show voice-leading, cadence and range checks rather than output alone.
- Attach prescribed works to an examination-cycle field so they can be replaced without changing
  transferable theory and aural skills.
- Generate original exercises and commentary; do not reproduce SimpleStudy answers or copyrighted
  scores.

## Coverage cautions

- Prescribed works change by cycle. Verify the target year before generating listening content.
- Musical notation and bar references require visual validation after rendering.
