# Music worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy uses technique checklists, sectioned analysis and note callouts. Follow that teaching
shape only. SEC marking schemes and prescribed-work notices govern the target response; generate
original exercises and avoid reproducing copyrighted scores.

## Required answer shape

1. Identify the musical feature or compositional requirement.
2. Locate audible or notated evidence precisely.
3. Name the technique using correct terminology.
4. Explain its structural, expressive or stylistic effect.
5. Check voice-leading, range, cadence, rhythm and notation where composing.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** Explain why a V-I cadence creates closure in a major key.

**Model shape:** The dominant chord contains the leading note, which tends to rise to the tonic,
while scale degree two commonly resolves to the tonic or third. Root movement from dominant to
tonic reinforces the home key. In a generated harmonisation, verify that the voices avoid parallel
fifths and octaves while making these resolutions clearly.
