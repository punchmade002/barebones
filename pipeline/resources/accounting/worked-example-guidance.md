# Accounting worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy combines concise rules, note callouts and stepwise numerical applications. Follow that
teaching pattern only. SEC papers and marking schemes control formats and marks; all figures,
businesses and solutions must be original.

## Required answer shape

1. Identify the account, statement or principle required.
2. Classify each relevant figure and adjustment.
3. Show the calculation and both sides/effects where double entry applies.
4. Place the result in the correct statement and period.
5. Check balancing, subtotals and the accounting equation.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** Equipment cost €24,000, residual value €4,000 and useful life five years.
Calculate one year’s straight-line depreciation.

**Model shape:** Depreciable amount = €24,000 - €4,000 = €20,000. Annual depreciation = €20,000 / 5
= €4,000. Charge €4,000 to the income statement and increase accumulated depreciation by €4,000;
the two effects preserve the double-entry relationship.
