# Home Economics worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy uses sectioned factual explanation with note callouts. Applied questions should turn
those facts into a justified household decision. SEC papers and marking schemes define credit;
households, values and recommendations must be original.

## Required answer shape

1. Identify the person, household or consumer need.
2. State the relevant principle.
3. Apply it to specific scenario evidence.
4. Show any calculation and assumptions.
5. Give a feasible recommendation with a consequence or trade-off.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** A household has €420 available after fixed bills and wants to build an
emergency fund while controlling food costs.

**Model shape:** Allocate savings first, for example €60, leaving €360 for variable needs. Set a
weekly food ceiling based on the remaining period, plan meals before shopping and compare unit
prices rather than pack prices. The €60 transfer is measurable and protects the saving goal, while
the food ceiling must still reflect household size and dietary needs.
