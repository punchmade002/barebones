## Pipeline guidance

- Present applied material as: need or constraint, relevant principle, justified choice,
  consequence and evaluation.
- Nutrition answers must connect nutrient/source/function/deficiency or excess to the stated
  person or life stage.
- Consumer and financial scenarios must show assumptions and calculations before recommendations.
- Keep factual knowledge, practical application and evaluation separately taggable.
- Generate original households, menus and consumer scenarios; do not reproduce SimpleStudy prose
  or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- Family law, consumer rights, social supports, prices and nutrition guidance are time-sensitive.
  Verify them against current Irish primary sources before publication.
