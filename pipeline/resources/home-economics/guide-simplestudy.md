# Home Economics research guide

## Source

- Provider: SimpleStudy, authenticated Higher Level course.
- Accessed: 2026-08-07.
- Coverage observed: 12 units and 32 topics.
- Representative lesson: “Sociological Terms and Concepts” (9 main sections, 11 subsections and
  3 note callouts).
- Syllabus: the Leaving Certificate Home Economics – Scientific and Social syllabus (© 2001),
  introduced September 2002, first examined 2004 and examined in June 2027. Do not use the draft
  redeveloped Leaving Certificate Home Economics specification (consultation February–April 2026;
  introduction from September 2027 at the earliest) or Junior Cycle Home Economics content.
- Use the taxonomy below as the single secondary research map. NCCA controls scope; SEC papers and
  marking schemes control examination content.
- The full chapter-by-chapter outline used for content generation, keyed to the app's 31 home
  economics chapter IDs, with Higher Level-only markings read from the syllabus PDF, is
  `course-outlines/homeeconomics.md`.

## Unit and topic structure

1. **The Family** — Sociological Terms and Concepts; The Family in Society; The Family as a Caring
   Unit.
2. **Marriage and Family Law** — Marriage; Family Law.
3. **Household Finances** — Housing; Budgeting; Insurance; Management of Household Financial
   Resources; Payment Options and Credit; Resource Management; Savings.
4. **Consumer Studies** — Role of Advertising; Household Income and Spending Patterns; Consumer
   Choice; Consumer Research; Consumer Rights and Responsibilities; Consumer Protection; Consumer
   Responsibility to the Environment.
5. **Nutrition — Protein** — Alternative Protein Foods.
6. **Nutrition — Vitamins and Minerals** — Nutrition and Energy; Minerals; Vitamins.
7. **Diet and Health** — Food Choices and Irish Food; Diet and Health.
8. **Meal Planning and Preparation** — Food Preparation.
9. **Foods** — Food Commodities and Extras; Milk and Milk Products.
10. **Food Safety, Spoilage and Hygiene** — Food Processing, Safety, and Hygiene; Microbiology.
11. **Household Appliances** — Household Technology.
12. **Textiles** — Textiles Overview.

## Pipeline guidance

- Present applied material as: need or constraint, relevant principle, justified choice,
  consequence and evaluation.
- Nutrition answers must connect nutrient/source/function/deficiency or excess to the stated
  person or life stage.
- Consumer and financial scenarios must show assumptions and calculations before recommendations.
- Keep factual knowledge, practical application and evaluation separately taggable.
- Generate original households, menus and consumer scenarios; do not reproduce SimpleStudy prose
  or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- Family law, consumer rights, social supports, prices and nutrition guidance are time-sensitive.
  Verify them against current Irish primary sources before publication.
- SimpleStudy's 32 topics cover only part of the syllabus. It has no topics for carbohydrate and
  lipid chemistry, digestion, water, the Irish food industry, food legislation, preservation in
  detail, appliance working principles, or the three electives beyond a housing topic and a
  textiles overview. Take scope for those from the syllabus via the course outline.
- Higher Level-only syllabus content is shown only by print colour (black) in the PDF; plain-text
  extraction loses it. Use the HL markings in the course outline.
- Do not generate Junior Cycle household-skills content (stain removal, garment storage and
  repair, home safety and security, functions of the home): it is not on this syllabus and has
  not been examined at Leaving Certificate.
