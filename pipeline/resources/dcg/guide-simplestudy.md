## Pipeline guidance

- Map SimpleStudy’s detailed navigation onto NCCA Plane and Descriptive Geometry, Communication of
  Design and Computer Graphics, and Applied Graphics.
- A worked solution must state givens, construction sequence, projection/view used, intersections
  or loci established, and a final verification of geometry and line convention.
- Preserve diagrams as first-class evidence. Text without the associated construction must not be
  treated as a solved example.
- Keep manual construction, freehand communication and CAD/student-assignment guidance distinct.
- Generate original dimensions and drawings; do not reproduce SimpleStudy worked examples.

  assignment and written examination must still be scaffolded from the official syllabus.
