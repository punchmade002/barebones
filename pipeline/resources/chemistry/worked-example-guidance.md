# Chemistry worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy mixes compact conceptual sections with worked cues for quantitative ideas. Reproduce
the learning pattern only. SEC papers and marking schemes govern questions and credit; equations,
values, explanations and solutions generated for the app must be original.

## Required answer shape

1. Write the governing equation or relationship.
2. Identify known quantities with units.
3. Rearrange or establish the mole ratio before substitution.
4. Show one justified operation per line.
5. Give a chemically sensible result with units, significant figures and a reasonableness check.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** Find the amount of substance in 5.85 g of sodium chloride, using
\(M_r = 58.5\).

**Model shape:** Use \(n=m/M_r\). Therefore \(n=5.85\text{ g}/58.5\text{ g mol}^{-1}=0.100\text{
mol}\). The mass is one tenth of the molar mass, so the magnitude of the answer is consistent.
