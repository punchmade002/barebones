## Unit and topic structure

1. **Atomic Structure** — Atomic Structure.
2. **The Periodic Table** — The Periodic Table; Families and Trends in the Periodic Table.
3. **Chemical Bonding** — Chemical Bonding and Structure; Electronegativity and the Continuum of
   Chemical Bonding.
4. **Stoichiometry and Quantitative Chemistry** — Law of Conservation of Mass: Introducing
   Stoichiometry; Limiting Reactants and Percentage Yield; The Mole.
5. **Gas Laws and Properties** — Kinetic Theory of Matter and Behaviour of Gases.
6. **Chemical Equilibrium** — Chemical Equilibrium.
7. **Rates of Reactions** — Rates of Reactions.
8. **Acids, Bases and pH** — Acids and Bases; pH and Indicators.
9. **Thermochemistry** — Thermochemistry.
10. **Oxidation, Reduction and Electrochemistry** — Oxidation and Reduction; Electrochemistry.
11. **Organic Chemistry** — Allotropes of Carbon and Hydrocarbons; More Families of Organic
    Compounds; Reactions of Organic Compounds.
12. **Environmental Chemistry** — Environmental Chemistry.
13. **Volumetric Analysis** — Acid-Base; Oxidation-Reduction.
14. **Experiments and Investigations** — flame tests; anion detection; relative molecular mass of
    a volatile liquid; conservation of mass; concentration decrease; preparing sodium carbonate;
    standardising hydrochloric acid, sodium hydroxide, potassium permanganate and sodium
    thiosulfate; ammonia and vinegar concentration; iron in a tablet; rate effects of hydrogen
    peroxide decomposition, surface area, concentration and temperature; equilibrium changes;
    heats of combustion and neutralisation; galvanic cells and inert-electrode electrolysis;
    ethene; ester; soap; aldehyde/ketone distinction; benzoic acid.
15. **Basics and Assessment** — How Scientists Work; Scientific Ideas and Science as a Global
    Enterprise; Models in Chemistry and Chemistry in Society; Written Exam; Chemistry in Practice
    Investigation.

## Pipeline guidance

- Attach every calculation to the governing relationship, balanced equation, units and
  significant-figure decision.
- Explain particle-level mechanisms alongside observable changes and symbolic equations.
- Experimental content must include purpose, apparatus/reagents, safety, controlled variables,
  method, observations/data, calculation and conclusion.
- Treat named investigations and Chemistry in Practice assessment as first-class nodes.
- Generate original explanations and numerical values; do not reproduce SimpleStudy prose or
  worked answers.

## Coverage cautions

- Chemical notation, state symbols, charges, units and balanced equations must survive extraction
  and rendering unchanged. Reject malformed scientific notation before merge.
