# English worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy uses layered explanation, “note/important” callouts and response planning for English.
Follow that instructional pattern only. SEC papers and marking schemes govern the task and credit;
prompts, passages and model prose must be original or appropriately licensed.

## Required answer shape

1. Parse the command word, audience, purpose and form.
2. Make a direct interpretive or compositional choice.
3. Support analysis with a precise textual reference or develop the chosen form consistently.
4. Explain technique and effect rather than listing features.
5. Link each paragraph to the task and maintain controlled expression.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** Explain the shape of a paragraph analysing how a writer creates tension.

**Model shape:** Begin with a specific claim about the tension, identify a short textual detail,
name the relevant technique only if that helps the explanation, and analyse how its pace, sound,
imagery or withheld information affects the reader. End by linking the effect to the scene’s wider
conflict. Substitute verified evidence from the supplied passage; never invent a quotation.
