## Pipeline guidance

- Separate transferable skills and response formats from prescribed authors and texts.
- Tag responses by paper/section, mode, command word, text, technique and evidence type.
- Build analytical paragraphs as claim, precise textual reference, technique, effect and link to
  the question; personal response must remain evidence-based.
- Writing guidance should specify audience, purpose, register, form and structural choices.
- Generate original prompts and commentary; do not reproduce SimpleStudy prose or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- Prescribed texts and poets vary by examination year. The pipeline must bind text-specific nodes
  to the SEC prescribed-material list for the target year, not treat this snapshot as permanent.
- Quotation accuracy and copyright limits require special validation. Prefer short textual
  references and analysis; never fabricate quotations.
