## Pipeline guidance

- Link every model, diagram and calculation to its assumptions and ceteris-paribus conditions.
- Build responses as concept, mechanism, diagram/data evidence, applied context and evaluation.
- Treat the Research Study as a separate assessment workflow requiring source quality, data
  interpretation and justified conclusions.
- Verify current indicators, budgets and policy rates from primary sources at generation time.
- Generate original examples and datasets; do not reproduce SimpleStudy prose or answers.
