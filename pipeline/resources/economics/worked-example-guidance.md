# Economics worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy uses sectioned concepts, short applications and note callouts. Follow the presentation
rhythm, not its wording. SEC marking schemes determine reward points; examples and datasets must be
independently created and time-stamped where current data is used.

## Required answer shape

1. Define the relevant concept and assumptions.
2. Explain the causal mechanism.
3. Use a labelled diagram, calculation or data item where relevant.
4. Apply the mechanism to the scenario.
5. Evaluate limitations, distributional effects or alternative outcomes.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** Explain one likely effect of a binding maximum rent below equilibrium.

**Model shape:** At the controlled price, quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied, creating a
shortage. Existing tenants who secure housing pay less, but some landlords may withdraw units or
reduce investment. The final effect depends on enforcement, supply responsiveness and whether
complementary housing policies increase capacity.
