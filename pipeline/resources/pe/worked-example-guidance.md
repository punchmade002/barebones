# Physical Education worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy uses short conceptual sections and prominent note callouts for performance concepts.
Follow the structure, not the wording. SEC papers and marking schemes determine exam requirements;
examples and analysis must be original.

## Required answer shape

1. Name and define the relevant performance principle.
2. Cite an observable performance indicator.
3. Explain how the indicator affects the activity outcome.
4. Propose a specific, progressive intervention.
5. State how improvement will be measured and reviewed.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** A badminton player is repeatedly late to the shuttle at the front court.
Suggest one justified intervention.

**Model shape:** Video observations show the player remains upright after the opponent strikes,
delaying the first step. The player should practise split-step timing in short, low-fatigue feeding
drills, then add unpredictable feeds. Success can be tracked as the percentage of front-court
shuttles contacted above net height across three comparable sessions.
