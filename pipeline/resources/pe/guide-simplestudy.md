## Pipeline guidance

- Preserve the theory units and physical-activity areas as separate scaffold dimensions so a
  question can be tagged by both concept and activity context.
- Present theory as: definition, relevant principle, performance evidence, application, and
  justified improvement.
- Performance-analysis tasks must name an observable indicator and connect it to a realistic
  training or practice intervention.
- Keep project, performance and written-exam guidance distinct.
- Generate original examples; do not reproduce SimpleStudy prose or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- Examples must not imply that one training method is universally suitable. Include participant
  needs, activity demands, safety and progression.
