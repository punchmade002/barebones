## Pipeline guidance

- Keep concept, system, experiment and assessment nodes distinct. Link experiments back to the
  biological principle they test.
- Explain mechanisms as a causal sequence, naming structures, substances and direction of change.
- Experimental content must include question/hypothesis, variables, control, method, measurement,
  safety, results and evidence-based conclusion.
- Diagrams must have functional labels rather than decorative captions.
- Generate original explanations and experimental scenarios; do not reproduce SimpleStudy prose
  or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- “Bacteria — Microorganisms” and “Viruses — Classification of Life” are SimpleStudy navigation
  labels, not claims that bacteria are the only microorganisms or that viruses are living
  organisms. The NCCA specification governs the scientific treatment.
