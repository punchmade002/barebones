## Unit and topic structure

1. **Algebra: Expressions and Fractions** — Expressions; Factorising; Algebraic Fractions; Binomial
   Expansions; Long Division in Algebra.
2. **Algebra: Equations** — Linear Equations; Simultaneous Linear Equations in Two or Three
   Variables; Quadratic Equations; Linear/Non-Linear Simultaneous Equations; Factor Theorem;
   Manipulation of Formulae; Unknown Coefficients; Algebraic Problem Solving.
3. **Algebra: Inequalities** — Surd Equations and Linear Inequalities; Quadratic and Rational
   Inequalities; Absolute Value; Inequality Proofs; Discriminants.
4. **Length, Area, and Volume** — Two-Dimensional Shapes; Rectangular Solids and Prisms; Cylinders,
   Cones, Spheres, and Hemispheres; Trapezoidal Rule.
5. **Real Numbers** — Factors, Multiples, and Prime Factors; Integers and Rational Numbers;
   Irrational Numbers; Rounding and Significant Figures; Orders of Magnitude and Scientific
   Notation.
6. **Functions** — Functions, Mapping, and Composition; Linear and Quadratic Functions; Completed
   Square Form; Cubic Functions; Exponential Functions; Logarithmic Graphs; Injective, Surjective,
   and Bijective Functions; Inverse Functions; Function/Inverse Graphs.
7. **Indices and Logarithms** — Laws of Indices; Equations with an Unknown Index; Surds;
   Logarithms; Practical Logarithm Problems.
8. **Number Patterns, Sequences, and Series** — Patterns; Arithmetic Sequences; Arithmetic Series;
   Nonlinear Sequences; Geometric Sequences and Series; Infinite Series.
9. **Arithmetic** — Approximation, Percentage Error, and Tolerance; Costing; Income Tax; VAT.
10. **Financial Mathematics** — Present Value; Compound Interest; Reducing-Balance Depreciation;
    Geometric-Series Applications.
11. **Proof by Induction** — Proof and Summations; Induction; Series Proofs; Divisibility Proofs;
    Inequalities.
12. **Complex Numbers** — Introduction; Argand Diagram and Modulus; Addition/Subtraction and Real
    Scalar Multiplication; Multiplication/Division; Complex Roots; Polar Form; De Moivre’s Theorem.
13. **Differential Calculus: Techniques** — Limits and Continuity; First Principles; Polynomial
    and Rational-Power Differentiation; Chain Rule; Product and Quotient Rules; Trigonometric,
    Inverse-Trigonometric, Exponential and Natural-Log Differentiation; Implicit Differentiation.
14. **Differential Calculus: Applications** — Second Derivative; Increasing/Decreasing Functions;
    Turning Points and Inflection; Optimisation; Rates of Change.
15. **Integral Calculus** — Antiderivatives; Sums/Differences/Constant Multiples; Exponential and
    Trigonometric Integrals; Definite Integrals; Area; Intersecting Curves and Trapezoidal Rule;
    Average Value.
16. **Statistics: Data and Correlation** — Data Types; Sample Surveys; Data Collection and Tables;
    Graphs; Scatter Graphs and Correlation; Linear Regression.
17. **Statistics: Descriptive Statistics** — Measures of Centre; Choice of Average; Variation;
    Relative Standing; Normal Distribution and Empirical Rule.
18. **Statistics: Inferential Statistics** — Sampling; Confidence Interval for a Mean; Population
    Proportion; Hypothesis Testing.
19. **Probability: Foundations** — Counting Principle; Combinations; Relative Frequency and
    Experimental Probability; Equally Likely Outcomes; Probability Theory; Conditional
    Probability.
20. **Probability: Distributions** — Sequential and Independent Events; Tree Diagrams; Expected
    Value; Binomial Distribution; Normal Distribution and Applications.
21. **Geometry** — Basic Concepts, Angles, and Axioms; Angles and Lines; Triangle Properties;
    Quadrilaterals; Triangle/Parallelogram Area; Further Triangle Properties; Circles.
22. **Geometry Proofs** — Proof Introduction; Congruent Triangles; Proofs.
23. **Trigonometry** — Right Triangles and Ratios; Practical Problems; Degrees and Radians; Unit
    Circle; Trigonometric Graphs; Transformed Graphs; Special Angles; Equations; Sine Rule; Cosine
    Rule; Triangle Area; Arc/Sector; Three-Dimensional Problems; Formula Derivations and
    Applications.
24. **Co-ordinate Geometry: The Line** — Formula Revision; Division of a Segment; Equation of a
    Line; Triangle Area; Point-to-Line Distance; Inclination and Angle Between Lines.
25. **Co-ordinate Geometry: The Circle** — Centre at Origin; Centre at (h,k); General Forms;
    Inside/Outside/On a Circle; Line-Circle Intersection; Tangents and Touching Circles; Mixed
    Problems.
26. **Constructions** — Equipment and Introduction; Constructions 16–22.
27. **Enlargements** — Transformations; Properties of Enlargements.
