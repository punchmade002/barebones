# Politics and Society worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy uses layered concepts, thinker links, contemporary applications and note callouts.
Follow that structure without reproducing notes. SEC marking schemes determine credit; political
facts, data and examples must be independently verified.

## Required answer shape

1. State a direct claim using the relevant concept.
2. Explain a named thinker’s argument accurately where useful.
3. Apply reliable evidence or data to the question.
4. Consider a competing interpretation or limitation.
5. Reach a reasoned conclusion tied to the prompt.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** Evaluate whether rules are legitimate merely because they are formally made.

**Model shape:** Formal procedure can support legitimacy through predictability and accountability,
but procedure alone is insufficient if affected groups lack meaningful participation or rights are
violated. Compare the rule-making process, the authority’s mandate, the rule’s impact and available
appeal mechanisms before reaching a judgement. Use a verified case rather than a fabricated one.
