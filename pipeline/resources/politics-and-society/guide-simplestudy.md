# Politics and Society research guide

## Source

- Provider: SimpleStudy, authenticated Higher Level course and current public lesson catalogue.
- Accessed: 2026-08-07.
- Coverage observed: 10 units and 57 topics.
- Representative lesson: “Key Concepts” (7 main sections, 37 subsections and 7 note/important
  callouts).
- Use the taxonomy below as a secondary navigation map. NCCA controls scope and assessment; SEC

## Unit and topic structure

1. **Power and Decision-Making in School** — Processes of Power and Decision-Making in Schools;
   Need for Rules; Concept of Power; Effects of Rules and Rule-Making Processes.
2. **Power and Decision-Making at National and European Level** — Making of National Policy;
   Selection of the Executive Branch; Social Class and Gender; Effectiveness of Representation;
   Traditional and New Media in a Democracy.
3. **Effectively Contributing to Communities** — People Who Positively Impact Society; Involvement
   in Groups and Organisations; Taking Action; Goals and Action Plans; Personal Qualities;
   Evaluation.
4. **Rights and Responsibilities in Communication with Others** — Freedom of Expression;
   Listening and Communicating; Difference, Negotiation and Conflict Resolution; Seeking and
   Evaluating Information and Ideas; Democratic Participation.
5. **Human Rights and Responsibilities in Ireland** — Rights of Young People; Human Rights
   Principles; Equality; Arguments About Rights; State Bodies for Human Rights; Right to Education.
6. **Human Rights and Responsibilities in Europe and the Wider World** — Rights in the Wider
   World; Arguments About Rights; International Cooperation and Human Rights.
7. **Globalisation and Identity** — Globalisation and Identity.
8. **Sustainable Development** — Sustainable Development Actions; Sustainable Development
   Arguments.
9. **Key Thinkers** — Benedict Anderson; Kwame Anthony Appiah; Noam Chomsky; Thomas Hylland
   Eriksen; André Gunder Frank; Paulo Freire; Thomas Hobbes; Samuel Huntington; John Locke;
   Kathleen Lynch; Karl Marx; Seán McDonagh; Robert Nozick; Martha Nussbaum; Edward Said; Vandana
   Shiva; Sylvia Walby; Max Weber; Hannah Arendt; Émile Durkheim; Jean-Jacques Rousseau.
10. **Assessment** — Citizenship Project; Written Exam; Types of Question; Key Concepts.

## Pipeline guidance

- Map the first eight units to the NCCA’s four strands and keep key thinkers as linked lenses, not
  isolated biography chapters.
- Build responses as claim, concept/thinker, reliable evidence or data, counter-position and
  reasoned conclusion.
- Separate descriptive political facts from normative arguments and disclose the evidence base.
- Citizenship Project guidance must include question, participation, action plan, evidence,
  reflection and evaluation.
- Generate original prose and scenarios; do not reproduce SimpleStudy notes or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- Institutions, office-holders, law, human-rights cases and social statistics are time-sensitive.
  Verify them from current primary sources before publication.
