# Art worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy uses period/artist sections, visual-analysis prompts and note callouts. Follow that
structure without copying essays or unlicensed images. SEC criteria govern assessment and NCCA’s
Research, Create and Respond strands govern the process.

## Required answer shape

1. Identify the artwork/process and answer the precise prompt.
2. Refer to specific visible evidence: composition, material, colour, form, scale or technique.
3. Explain how the evidence creates meaning or effect.
4. Connect it to verified context without replacing visual analysis with biography.
5. Form a supported personal judgement or document reflection and refinement.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** Show the structure of a paragraph analysing how an artwork creates emphasis.

**Model shape:** Make a claim about the focal area, cite two observable choices such as contrast and
placement, explain how the viewer’s eye is directed, and connect that emphasis to the work’s theme.
Use only evidence visible in the licensed image supplied to the question; never invent details.
