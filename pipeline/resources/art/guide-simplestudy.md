## Pipeline guidance

- Make Research, Create and Respond cross-cutting tags on every practical and Visual Studies node.
- Artist and period responses should cover what was created, how it was created, why/context and a
  supported personal response using precise visual evidence.
- Practical guidance must document stimulus, primary research, experimentation, material decisions,
  refinement, realised work and reflection in the sketchpad process.
- Use images only when their licence/provenance is recorded; never infer visual details from a title.
- Generate original analysis and project prompts; do not reproduce SimpleStudy essays or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- The public catalogue contains overlapping period, theme and artist cards rather than a single
  official linear chapter order. The NCCA three-strand specification is the scaffold authority.
- Contemporary topics and artist selections may change; record access date and image rights.
