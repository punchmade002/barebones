6. **Assessment** — Written Exam; Research Study.

## Pipeline guidance

- Store topic, chronology, named person/event, document/source and case-study status separately.
- Build paragraphs as claim, precise evidence, analysis tied to the question, and a short
  judgement or transition.
- Distinguish primary-source evidence from later interpretation; never invent quotations.
- Long answers need an explicit line of argument and selected evidence, not a chronology dump.
- Generate original prose; do not reproduce SimpleStudy notes or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- Preserve date ranges and geographical qualifiers. Flag uncertain dates, casualty figures and
  attributed motives for primary-source verification.
