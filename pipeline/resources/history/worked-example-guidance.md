# History worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy uses chronological sections, named evidence and note callouts. Follow that navigational
clarity without reproducing notes. SEC papers and marking schemes determine the response type and
credit; wording, argument and source analysis must be original.

## Required answer shape

1. Answer the precise question with a defensible claim.
2. Supply accurate, selected evidence.
3. Explain how the evidence supports the claim.
4. Add qualification or comparison where relevant.
5. Return explicitly to the line of argument.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** Show the shape of a paragraph evaluating the importance of a political
leader.

**Model shape:** Begin with a bounded claim about one contribution, then supply a dated action or
development, explain its effect on organisation, public opinion or policy, and weigh that effect
against a named limitation. End by stating whether the evidence makes the contribution decisive,
important but partial, or secondary. Replace every placeholder with verified topic evidence; never
invent a quotation.
