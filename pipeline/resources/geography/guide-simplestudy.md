# Geography research guide

## Source

- Provider: SimpleStudy, authenticated Higher Level course.
- Accessed: 2026-10-15, from a screen recording of the course's unit/topic sidebar.
- Coverage observed: 27 units and 57 topics. Third-level topics are listed where the recording
  showed them.
- Representative lesson: "The Earth's Structure" (5 main sections and 2 subsections).
- Syllabus: the Leaving Certificate Geography syllabus first examined in 2006 and examined to
  June 2027. SimpleStudy follows this syllabus, so the taxonomy below is aligned with it.
- This taxonomy is a secondary research map. The NCCA syllabus controls course scope, and SEC
  papers and marking schemes control exam material.
- The full chapter-by-chapter outline used for content generation, keyed to the app's 25
  geography chapter IDs, is `course-outlines/geography.md`.

## Pipeline guidance

- Represent each item as a concept/skill node, and record place examples, case studies, map
  skills and investigation skills separately.
- Build explanations as: process, named evidence/example, spatial pattern, consequence, and
  evaluation where required. Higher Level 30-mark answers are marked as 15 significant relevant
  points (SRPs), so notes must be dense with distinct, specific facts.
- Map and photograph answers must cite visible evidence and location before inference.
- For case studies, generate a claim-evidence-link structure, and verify statistics and dates
  from current primary sources.
- Generate original explanations. Do not reproduce SimpleStudy prose or answers.

## Coverage cautions

- **Do not use the 2026 specification.** The NCCA *Curriculum Specification for Leaving
  Certificate Geography* (introduced September 2026, first examined 2028) is a different course.
  Do not generate its topics (e.g. geopolitics and territorial disputes, the four EU freedoms,
  Irish Aid, layers of the atmosphere, Ireland's temperate rainforest, urban food production,
  smart cities, Mediterranean mass tourism as a set case study, the Applied Geography Project).
- Electives are alternatives: students answer one of Elective 4 or Elective 5, never both.
- Options are Higher Level only, and students answer one. The syllabus marks no other topic as
  Higher Level only.
- `spec-syllabus.pdf` is the official 2003 NCCA syllabus. Where SimpleStudy and the syllabus
  differ, the syllabus wins: see Appendix A of `course-outlines/geography.md` for topics the
  syllabus requires that SimpleStudy does not cover (e.g. wind erosion, human interaction with
  the rock cycle, fluvial adjustment to base level, GIS studies, migration policy, the future of
  urbanism, human alteration of biomes).
