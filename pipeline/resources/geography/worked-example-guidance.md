# Geography worked-example guidance

## Source pattern

SimpleStudy’s descriptive lessons use short thematic sections. Use that pacing while grounding
scope in the NCCA specification and reward points in SEC marking schemes. Named examples, figures
and explanations must be independently written and verified.

## Required answer shape

1. Identify the process, skill or geographical claim.
2. Describe the mechanism or visible spatial evidence.
3. Support it with a relevant named example where required.
4. Explain effects at the appropriate scale.
5. Evaluate or compare only when the command word requires it.

## Original model

**Synthetic prompt:** Explain why earthquakes occur at a conservative plate boundary.

**Model shape:** Plates move laterally past one another, but friction can lock sections of the
boundary. Stress accumulates as plate motion continues. When the stress exceeds frictional
resistance, the rocks slip suddenly and release energy as seismic waves. Because crust is neither
created nor destroyed, earthquake risk is the main direct tectonic hazard at this boundary type.
