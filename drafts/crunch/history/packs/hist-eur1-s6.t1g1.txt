[hist-eur1-s1.0.3] Balance of power: a ring of buffer states was built around France: the Kingdom of the Netherlands, enlarged to include Belgium; Piedmont-Sardinia, strengthened by gaining Genoa; and a neutral Switzerland.
[hist-eur1-s1.0.4] Austria gave up the Austrian Netherlands but gained Lombardy-Venetia in northern Italy, and Habsburg relatives ruled Tuscany, Modena and Parma, making Austria the dominant power in the peninsula.
[hist-eur1-s1.0.5] Prussia gained the Rhineland and Westphalia, which made it guardian of the Rhine frontier against France, together with about two-fifths of Saxony and Swedish Pomerania.
[hist-eur1-s1.0.6] Russia gained most of the Duchy of Warsaw as the Congress Kingdom of Poland, with the Tsar as its king.
[hist-eur1-s1.0.7] Britain kept colonies and bases taken during the wars, including Malta, Heligoland, Ceylon and the Cape Colony, and Castlereagh pressed for a moderate peace that did not humiliate France.
