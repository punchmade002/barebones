  Q: Outline the significance of fatty acids in the diet.
  A: 3 points @ 3 marks each 
Unsaturated fatty acids (cis) are generally good as they help reduce LDL and increase 
HDL; etc. Trans fatty acids are believed to increase incidence of CHD – increasing levels of 
LDL, depositing cholesterol on the wall of the arteries; reduces HDL which removes

(12)

(9)
