      Tuning

       

      Exam value: each Higher Level paper from 2015–2025 (old course) is re-marked as if it were the 2027 paper. The old short questions are the compulsory Q1, and the long questions are the ones you choose from. Every exam part is tagged to the note points that earn its marks; half its marks go to those points and half spread over their topic, because new papers ask other points of the same topics. Positions are backtested: the cut is chosen from older papers and marked on newer ones, so the grade is what you’d really score. Subsections the old papers never tested are new-course content, given course-average value. Points in a loop (each needs the other) are bundled: kept or hidden together, with one shared value.
