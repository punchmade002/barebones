  1. 01

    Stakeholders, their importance and interdependence

    A stakeholder is any person or group affected by a business or able to affect it. Stakeholders are classed as internal or external according to their relationship with the business.

    • What a stakeholder is
    • Owners and investors
    • Employees and managers
    • Consumers and suppliers
    • Local community and government
    • How needs change from start-up to maturity

  2. 02

    Stakeholder interaction, conflict and its resolution

    Stakeholders interact in co-operative ways, where their interests align, and in competitive ways, where one group's gain is another's loss.

    • Co-operative and competitive relationships
    • Owners and employees: pay and working conditions
    • Business and the community: planning and pollution
    • Business and consumers: price and quality
    • Business and suppliers: price and payment terms
    • Investors and management: risk and dividends

  3. 03

    Stakeholder mapping and prioritising stakeholder interests

    Stakeholder mapping identifies each stakeholder affected by a decision and records how much power and interest each one has. The map can be written or visual.

    • What stakeholder mapping is
    • The power-interest grid
    • Mapping a decision step by step
    • Why prioritise stakeholder interests
    • Limitations of stakeholder mapping
