# Q: Is the Maths exam bank larger than 160 questions?

## Answer

No hidden Maths exam bank exists. The live source contains 97 grouped questions and 160 answerable parts across all 26 chapters. Forty-six groups contain multiple parts. Coverage is limited to eight papers from 2022 through 2025: 55 Paper 1 groups and 42 Paper 2 groups. The interactive worked-solution renderer covers all 160 parts, but the historical Maths archive itself is light and should be expanded.
